The building, in 3D

The same four floors as the main page, built from boxes: basement archive, office and lobby, the library, and portals with the news room on top. One agent makes a round. Drag to turn it.

A sketch, not the replay: the main page's pixel view is driven by real traces, and this one walks a fixed loop. Rendering at a third of the resolution and scaling up with nearest-neighbour sampling is what makes it read as pixel art.
